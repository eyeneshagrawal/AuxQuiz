How to use AuxQuiz

Everything you need to go from a PDF to practice questions.

Getting started

  1. Create a free account and log in.
  2. Click Get Started to open Quiz Studio.
  3. Upload a text-based PDF (max 20 MB).
  4. Pick mode, number of questions and difficulty, then press Generate.

Modes

  • Quiz (MCQ): 4 options per question, instant feedback, a short explanation and a final score.
  • Questions & Answers: written questions with model answers. Reveal each answer, or all at once.
  • Flashcards: a question on the front, the answer on the back. Click or press Space to flip, use the arrow keys or buttons to move, and Shuffle to mix the deck.

Getting better questions

  • Use Focus topic (e.g. "Normalization") to target a chapter.
  • Use hard for deeper, application-style questions.
  • Generate again for a fresh set from the same PDF.

Your data

PDFs are read in your browser. Only the most relevant sections are sent to the AI. A copy of each PDF is saved to your upload history. Dashboard activity and preferences are stored locally on this device.

Why does my PDF say "No selectable text found"?

Scanned or image-only PDFs have no text layer and are not supported yet. Use a PDF where you can select text.

How many questions can I generate at once?

Up to 20 per generation: 5, 10, 15 or 20.

Are answers always correct?

Questions are generated only from your material, but AI can make mistakes. Check important answers against your notes.

Where is my score saved?

Quiz scores appear on your Dashboard and are stored on this device only, so they will not follow you to another browser.

The "Open" link in my upload history fails.

Ask the site admin to enable "PDF and ZIP files delivery" in Cloudinary security settings.
